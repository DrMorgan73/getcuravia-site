Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · International Empire Consultants, Inc. ("IEC, Inc."), operating the CURAVIA clinic platform (www.getcuravia.com).

The short version

Demo requests are mailto: links that open your own email app — nothing is transmitted to us by the site. The one exception is the mutual NDA signing flow described below: if you choose to sign, the details you enter are transmitted to IEC so the executed agreement can be recorded and emailed to both parties. We set no analytics cookies and run no tracking scripts.

NDA signing

If you sign the mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement on this site, the agreement details you enter (counterparty name, signer name, title, signature date, and email address), your typed signature, and your IP address are sent to IEC, Inc. IEC records the signing, generates the executed agreement as a PDF, and emails a copy to you and to IEC. We keep the signing record (including a cryptographic hash of the issued PDF) as evidence of execution. No patient information is involved in this flow — do not enter any.

Information you send us directly

If you email us at info@getcuravia.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use that information only to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

What the site does not do

  • No accounts, logins, or passwords on this site.
  • No analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party cookies set by this site.
  • Apart from an NDA you choose to sign, no form on this site posts data to us.

Future changes

If we ever add server-side data collection to this site (for example a contact-form backend or analytics), we will update this policy before that collection begins and describe what is collected and why.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@getcuravia.com.

Resumen en español

Las solicitudes de demostración son enlaces de correo que abren su propia aplicación de correo; el sitio no nos transmite nada. La única excepción es la firma del acuerdo mutuo de confidencialidad: si decide firmar, los datos que ingrese (nombre de la contraparte, nombre y cargo del firmante, fecha de firma y correo electrónico), su firma escrita y su dirección IP se envían a IEC, Inc., que registra la firma, genera el acuerdo ejecutado en PDF y envía una copia por correo a usted y a IEC. No incluya información de pacientes en este flujo. Si nos escribe a info@getcuravia.com, usaremos su mensaje solo para responderle. No vendemos ni compartimos su información.
