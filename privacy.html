Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · International Empire Consultants, Inc. ("IEC, Inc."), operating the CURAVIA clinic platform (www.getcuravia.com).

The short version

Demo requests are mailto: links that open your own email app — nothing is transmitted to us by the site. This site has no signing form: the mutual NDA is signed only on IEC's central signing page, as described below. We set no analytics cookies and run no tracking scripts.

NDA signing

IEC uses one central mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement for all of its assets, signed only at iecincglobal.com/cnda.html — this site carries no NDA form. If you sign there, the agreement details you enter (counterparty name, signer name, title, signature date, and email address), your typed signature, and your IP address are sent to IEC, Inc. IEC records the signing, generates the executed agreement as a PDF, and emails a copy to you and to IEC. IEC keeps the signing record (including a cryptographic hash of the issued PDF) as evidence of execution. No patient information is involved in that flow — do not enter any.

Information you send us directly

If you email us at info@getcuravia.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use that information only to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

What the site does not do

  • No accounts, logins, or passwords on this site.
  • No analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party cookies set by this site.
  • No form on this site posts data to us.

Future changes

If we ever add server-side data collection to this site (for example a contact-form backend or analytics), we will update this policy before that collection begins and describe what is collected and why.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@getcuravia.com.
