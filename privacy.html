Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026 · International Empire Consultants, Inc. ("IEC, Inc."), operating the CURAVIA clinic platform (www.getcuravia.com).

The short version

This website does not collect personal information through the site itself. Demo requests are mailto: links that open your own email app — nothing is transmitted to us by the site. The mutual NDA draft tool runs entirely in your browser; the signed draft downloads to your device and is never uploaded to our servers. We set no analytics cookies and run no tracking scripts.

Information you send us directly

If you email us at info@getcuravia.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use that information only to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

What the site does not do

  • No accounts, logins, or passwords on this site.
  • No server-side forms — there is no form on this site that posts data to us.
  • No analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party cookies set by this site.
  • The NDA tool's output never leaves your device.

Future changes

If we ever add server-side data collection to this site (for example a contact-form backend or analytics), we will update this policy before that collection begins and describe what is collected and why.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@getcuravia.com.
